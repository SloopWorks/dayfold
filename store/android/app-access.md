# Google Play app-access instructions

No pre-provisioned demo credentials are required. A reviewer can choose **Sign in
with Google**, then select **Create a family** to reach the complete app experience.

The core briefing remains usable if notifications, calendar access, camera access,
or location are denied. Account deletion is available under **Account → Delete
account**. Saved-place reminders are optional; the device's live location is
matched locally and is not uploaded.
