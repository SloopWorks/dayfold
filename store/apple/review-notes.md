# App Review notes

Dayfold does not require a pre-provisioned demo account. The reviewer can choose
Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, then select **Create a family** to reach
the complete app experience.

Suggested review path:

1. Sign in and create a family.
2. Open the sample briefing and event hub.
3. Open Account to confirm **Delete account** is available in app.
4. Open Settings to inspect optional notifications, calendar access, and saved-place
   reminders. Denying any permission leaves the core briefing available.

Precise live location is matched on the device and is not uploaded. A saved place
explicitly authored by the user may sync as family content. There are no ads or
in-app purchases, and standard HTTPS is the app's only encryption use.
