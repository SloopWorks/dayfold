The aggregate is derived, so its honest chip is “Matched on your device.” Its constituents are authored, so theirs read “Added by Claude.” Flattening those into one label would be a lie in whichever direction it went. Provenance attaches to the claim, not to the container

The group header makes exactly one claim — this condition matched — and carries exactly one chip for it. It never speaks for its children. Each child makes its own claim and carries its own chip. There is no group-level provenance, because a group is not a source.

The wall is avoided by weight, not by omission. While a child is collapsed to a row, its origin renders as the chip’s glyph alone — the same auto_awesome mark, in the same violet provenance role, without the pill and the words. Expand the child and the mark grows into the full chip. Nothing is ever hidden and nothing is ever merged; the same claim is simply said at the volume the row can carry.

One visible surface, one claim, one mark. Never two chips describing one thing, never one chip describing two.
