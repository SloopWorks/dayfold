Weather-conditional content

Dayfold is not becoming a weather app. Weather never produces a forecast readout — it qualifies content the family already cares about. The curator authors a card, attaches a condition and what to do about it; the device fetches a forecast for that place, decides whether the condition holds, and if it does the card surfaces with one quiet chip. If it doesn’t, the card isn’t there.

The bar these screens are built against: a family with a rained-out soccer game opens Dayfold and immediately knows what changed and what to do — without reading a forecast, without hunting, and without ever being shown weather the app didn’t confirm.
