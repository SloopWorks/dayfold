Every family, name, Hub, file and message in these prototypes is invented; no real user data appears anywhere. These pages contain no product network call, database, embedding, stored query, telemetry event, or app code, and the “search” never runs; like the neighboring .dc.html galleries, the prototype shell loads its runtime and fonts from a CDN. Results are drawn from the member's own already-synced cache, so nothing here shows content the active member could not already open; the operative privacy claim is that the search is local, not that the content never left a phone. Per ADR 0008 this gallery gates deeper planning, and sign-off on the design does not authorize the build. NOTES.md carries the state map, the M3 → Compose mapping, motion and accessibility notes, what was deliberately left out, and the open questions.
