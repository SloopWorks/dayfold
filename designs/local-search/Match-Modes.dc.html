Three ways to match, three honest things to say

Same corpus, three queries, three matchers. The interesting part is not the ranking — it is what each row is allowed to claim. A mark means the typed words are really there. A Close match means the row was reached through one typo, and the mark sits on the real word. A Related result means no typed word appears anywhere, so nothing is marked at all.

The production default is the first two boards. The third exists to be compared against them and, per the research report, to be measured before anyone ships it.
