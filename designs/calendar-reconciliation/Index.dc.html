Calendar Check — compare, review, decide

Dayfold compares structured Dayfold plans with calendars already on this phone — per member, per device, on the device. The calendar stays the schedule and owns generic event alerts; Dayfold owns selective preparation and curated Hubs. Nothing is ever written in either direction without an explicit confirmation: Dayfold → Calendar goes through the platform's native event editor, and Calendar → Dayfold is a reviewed, field-by-field import that starts private. Gaps collect into one calm review — never a notification, badge, or nag.
